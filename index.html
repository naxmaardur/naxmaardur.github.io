Games Models Mods

Magic of vile darkness

This mod project expands the amount of undead creatures in Skyrim to have more variety. The other objective for this mod was for me to learn how to convert models to Skyrim requiring me to learn how to rescale and rig. This together with Worn undead are two of my most downloaded mods that I made completely alone.

Total DLs 600,344

Worn undead

Worn undead is a continuation of the mod above but separated into another project because this mod adds undead versions for characters that already exist in the game but did not already have undead versions. For this project I modified the models of the base game to give all of them 6 to 9 different undead versions, coming to a total of 12 versions per character if we include its sister mod battle worn creatures.

Total DLs: 659,723

Immersive Death Cycle

Together with JaySerpa the models of Worn undead have been used again for a mod that makes dead creatures in skyrim appear like they decay over time.

Total DLs: 2,751,319

Nax ruins - Bleak falls barrow

This is part of a mod series in which I try to redesign Skyrim ruins/dungeons to better fit their lore, make them more unique and replayable. My primary focus with this redesign is to create a distinct difference between the temple section and when it becomes the burial chamber. I also tried to remove skyrims shortcut at the end of the dungeon trick and made the dungeon slightly change and spawn new enemies when you are leaving to make it feel like the undead are trying to prevent you from escaping with the dragon stone.

Nax ruins - Ranveigs fast

Another dungeon redesign mod. Ranveigs fast is a very forgettable location in the base game of Skyrim because it's a very generic dungeon with only one side quest and almost no content in it. I tried to improve it by increasing the prominence of this dungeon adding more various ways into it connected to other parts of the surrounding map. I also added a whole new sub dungeon area which is supposed to be a continuation of another forgotten side quest. This new dungeon section took heavy inspiration from how the dark souls 3 catacombs look and feel.
